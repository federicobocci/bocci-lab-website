Teaching


Our group supports several courses in Rabdoud’s Biology bachelor and Medical Biology master programs, including:

- Computation for Biologists (NWI-BM066A)

- Integrative Omics in practice (NWI-BM094)

- Computational Modelling for Molecular Biologists (NWI-BM093)

- Genomics and Big Data (NWI-BP031)

Additionally, Federico has supervised several students for Review article (NWI-BM-REVIEWART) and Research proposal (NWI-BM-RESPROP) writing.
