Contact & Join Us

Open positions and projects

We always look for motivated bachelor and master students with background in biology, physics, mathematics, computer science and life science in general. For inquiries, contact Federico at federico.bocci at ru.nl

- Bachelor internships are coordinated at the institute level, see more information on the Radboud institute for molecular life science education section.

- Master internships can be arranged by contacting Federico directly at federico.bocci at ru.nl.

- PhD/postdoc openings will be posted when available.

- Additionally, contact Federico if you are interested in applying for a Veni, Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions, or other PhD/postdoctoral fellowship.
